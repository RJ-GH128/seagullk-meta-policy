User Data Deletion Instructions｜用戶資料刪除說明

中文

如你希望刪除與 SeagullK Inbox Reader Test 相關的資料，請寄送電子郵件至：

Contact Email / 聯絡信箱： info@hcac.tw

主旨請填寫：SeagullK Inbox Reader Test－資料刪除要求

請提供足以協助我們辨識相關 App 使用紀錄的資訊，但請勿寄送密碼、Access Token、App Secret 或其他敏感憑證。

收到要求後，我們會：

  1. 確認要求是否與本應用程式相關；
  2. 檢查是否存在由本應用程式持久保存的相關資料；
  3. 若存在，於合理期間內刪除或匿名化；
  4. 回覆處理結果。

目前測試版本原則上不持久保存 Instagram 訊息原文、raw webhook payload、發送者／接收者識別資訊或 Meta 憑證。因此在多數情況下，可能沒有可供刪除的持久資料。

如你也希望移除 Instagram 或 Facebook 對本應用程式的授權，可透過相關 Meta / Instagram 帳號與應用程式設定取消授權。

English

To request deletion of data associated with SeagullK Inbox Reader Test, please email:

Contact Email: info@hcac.tw

Use the subject: SeagullK Inbox Reader Test – Data Deletion Request

Please provide enough information for us to identify any relevant App records. Do not send passwords, access tokens, App Secrets, or other sensitive credentials.

After receiving a request, we will:

  1. verify that the request relates to the App;
  2. check whether any relevant data has been persistently stored;
  3. delete or anonymize such data, if any, within a reasonable period;
  4. provide confirmation of the outcome.

The current test version generally does not persist Instagram message content, raw webhook payloads, sender or recipient identifiers, or Meta credentials. Therefore, in many cases there may be no persistent App data to delete.

You may also revoke the App’s access through the relevant Meta / Instagram account and application settings.
